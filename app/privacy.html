Privacy policy of kidev's Mod Loader

Effective September 29, 2026

kidev's Mod Loader (the "app") installs and plays mods for The Coffin of Andy and Leyley without changing the game itself. It is made by kidev, an individual developer (the "developer", "we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. It also covers the mod installers built from the same program, which other mod authors publish under their own mod's name.

In short

  • The app does not collect, store or share any personal information, and the developer receives none.
  • There is no account or sign-in, and there are no analytics, no ads, no crash reports and no tracking of any kind.
  • What the app remembers stays on your computer, and you can delete it at any time.
  • The app connects to the internet only to fetch mods and information about them, as described below.

Information we collect

None. The developer runs no server that the app reports to, and the app sends no information about you, your computer or your use of it to the developer or to anyone else.

When the app connects to the internet

The app downloads files over secure (https) connections, and only for these purposes:

  • The list of community mods, from tcoaal.app, or from raw.githubusercontent.com if that fails.
  • Details, pictures and downloads of mods, and checks for newer versions of an installed mod, from GitHub (api.github.com, github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com and the addresses GitHub sends downloads from).
  • Web addresses a mod's author chose, when a mod keeps its picture, its download or its updates somewhere other than GitHub.

These requests only ask for files. They carry no identifier and no information about you. Like any connection on the internet, they let the server you connect to see your IP address and basic technical details of the request, such as the time and the file asked for. Those services handle that under their own privacy policies: tcoaal.app is hosted on GitHub Pages, so for it and for GitHub this is the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive this information.

Links in the app (such as a mod's web page) open in your default web browser, which then works under its own privacy settings.

Information kept on your computer

To work, the app keeps a few things on your computer. None of it is sent anywhere.

  • In your game folder, a folder named addons: the mods you installed, which of them are enabled and in what order, and a modded copy of the game that it launches. The game's own files are never changed.
  • In your user folders (%APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA% on Windows): the location of your game folder, so the app does not have to search for it again; the last list of community mods, so it opens quickly; the program files the app unpacks to run; and small preferences such as whether the music is muted and which mod's page is shown.

To find your game and to launch it, the app reads where Steam is installed and which game folders it knows, and checks whether Steam is running. It only reads this on your computer; it does not send it anywhere.

Mods, the game and Steam

Mods are made by their authors, not by the developer, and a mod installed with the app, including the page the app shows for it, is their work. A mod's page may display content from the internet chosen by its author. Only install mods you trust.

When you press Play, the app starts the game you own through Steam. The game and Steam are not part of the app and have their own privacy policies, including the Valve Privacy Policy.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling a mod in the app removes it from the addons folder. To remove everything the app keeps, uninstall the app, then delete the addons folder in your game folder and the app's folders under %APPDATA% and %LOCALAPPDATA%. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Children

The app is meant for players of The Coffin of Andy and Leyley, a game for mature audiences, and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and its effective date changed. The page's history is public in the app's source repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be asked on the project's GitHub issues page.
