Players see all of this before they install, and the installer uses it to name itself.

Lowercase letters, digits and dashes, 3 to 40 characters. It becomes a folder on the player's disk and the prefix on their save files, so if you change it later the loader sees a different mod rather than an update to this one.

Functions your theme can call

    Build

    Each modded copy is compared against the build it came from, and only the differences go into the .tcoaalmod file. Nothing from the game itself travels inside it.

    Hand your players an installer

    One file per system, with your Customize screen inside it. A player downloads it, double-clicks it, and it finds their game on its own. There is nothing for them to unzip, place or configure. Build the ones your players are on.

    What the installer carries

    An online installer is a few hundred KB instead of your whole mod. It still wears your name, your icon and your Customize screen, and it downloads the mod when the player presses Install. Publish the .tcoaalmod first, then paste where it lives.
